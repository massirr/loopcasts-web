Changes after the frontend-design review

  • Two typefaces plus the handwriting: the monospace meta font is gone; numbers use the body font with even digit widths.
  • No middle dots or uppercase labels in course meta: "Beginner, 12 videos, 1 h 20 min" reads as plain text.
  • One animation that plays by itself (the hero workflow draws once). Everything else answers you: hover a card, press a button, finish an order.
  • Removed the card lift and the staggered fade-in, which are the most common generated-site effects.
  • The hero shows the site's own order workflow instead of a stock picture, so the slogan has proof next to it.
