# LoopCasts FAQ (public)

Source for the chat assistant (#22). Written from the support answers and the handbook (chapters 1, 3, 6), rewritten
for how the new website works. Public information only: no names, no customer details. The site serves this file at
/api/public/faq. Current courses and prices come from /api/public/courses and /api/public/plans.

## About LoopCasts
LoopCasts BV makes video courses about automation: n8n, webhooks, APIs, shell scripting, AI agents. If it saves
people from boring work, there is a course about it. We are a small team in Ghent, Belgium, making courses since 2019,
and we publish two new videos every week. Our customers are mostly developers, data people and small agencies.
All courses: /Courses.

## Plans and prices
All prices are excl. VAT. You see the total with VAT before you pay. Current plans and prices: /Pricing.
- Monthly subscription: all courses, billed every month.
- Yearly subscription: all courses for 12 months. The most popular plan.
- Team licence: all courses for 12 months, 5 seats, for companies.
- Single course: one course of your choice, lifetime access.
- Company workshop on site: on quote. Mail support@loopcasts.example.

## VAT
- Customers in Belgium pay 21% VAT.
- Private persons in other EU countries also pay 21%.
- Companies in another EU country with a valid VAT number pay 0%; the invoice says "VAT reverse charged".
- Customers outside the EU pay 0%.
The order form asks whether you order as a company and calculates it for you.

## Ordering and paying
1. Choose a plan on /Pricing and click Order.
2. Fill in the form: name, e-mail, country, and for companies the company name, VAT number and address.
   The team licence is for companies only.
3. Pay online. Right after the payment you get your invoice and your access; you don't have to wait.
4. On your first order you choose your password with the link on the confirmation page (also sent by e-mail,
   valid for 24 hours).

## Logging in and passwords
- You log in with the e-mail you ordered with: /Identity/Account/Login.
- Forgot your password? Click "Forgot your password?" on the login page. You get an e-mail with a link to choose
  a new one, valid for 24 hours. We never send passwords by e-mail and never ask for your password.
- Members of the old LoopCasts website: your access was moved to the new site. Use "Forgot your password?" with the
  e-mail you used before to choose a new password.
- You can turn on two-factor login (a code from an app on your phone) under Account settings.

## Invoices
- Your invoices are on "My courses" (after logging in): open one and use "Print or save as PDF".
- You also get the invoice by e-mail right after paying.
- Need a VAT number or a purchase order number added to an invoice? Mail support@loopcasts.example.

## Team licences
- A team licence has 5 seats: the buyer plus 4 colleagues.
- The buyer adds and removes colleagues on "My courses". A new colleague gets a link to choose a password.

## Renewals, cancelling and refunds
- Yearly subscriptions and team licences run for one year and do not renew automatically.
- About two weeks before your access ends you get an e-mail with a link to renew.
- Don't want to renew? Do nothing; your access stops on the end date.
- Want to cancel or ask for a refund? Mail support@loopcasts.example; we look at it personally.

## Videos
- A video doesn't play? It is nearly always a company firewall or an ad blocker that blocks Vimeo, where our videos
  are hosted. Try another network, or switch the ad blocker off for the LoopCasts website.
- Looking for the video that explains a specific topic, or hoping for a course about something new? Mail
  support@loopcasts.example; the team knows the catalogue by heart.

## Your data
- Under Account settings, Your data, you can download your data or delete your account. Invoices stay in our books,
  as the law requires.
- More about privacy: /Privacy.
